# Support

Certiv Labs tools are currently in internal beta and have no external support
commitment.

During internal hardening:

- use repository issues for reproducible bugs and documentation problems;
- use discussions or internal product channels for ideas and broad questions;
- use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Every public release must declare its maturity and maintenance level on its
tool page at [certiv.ai/tools](https://certiv.ai/tools/).
